# The gods and their champions

The gods send mortal champions into the arena to settle grudges. Each patron gives a gift that shapes its champion’s combat kit and demands a price that shapes their story. The selection screen carries these same champion profiles.

## Morvhal, God of Drowned Oaths

**Champion** · Edran Voss

The last captain of the drowned watch. Each life he saves adds another name to the shield that dragged him under.

Iron tide crown, barnacled pauldrons and a door ripped from a sunken burial vault.

## Ilyth, Goddess of Severed Fates

**Champion** · Sister Maer

She stitches the living to the dead and owes the Loom every hour she borrows. The thin ringing sound from her staff is a thread about to snap.

Ivory jaw veil, black funeral silk and a halo threaded with copper sutures.

## Vaesk, God of the Second Death

**Champion** · Sereth Twice-Buried

Vaesk demands two deaths. Sereth's shadow walks back through a killing stroke to collect the second.

Split funeral mask, broken orbit and two blades welded from opposite halves of a coffin nail.

## Hadrak, God of the Hungering Forge

**Champion** · Korr the Kilnborn

Korr keeps his heart in a travelling furnace. When the coals go cold, Hadrak asks him to feed it another memory.

Chimney spines, slag-crusted iron and a furnace built into his breastplate.

## Veyra, Goddess of the Final Flame

**Champion** · Ossa Wickbearer

The last flame ever lit cannot die, but every time Ossa moves it someone in the darkness discovers where she is.

Crooked iron reliquary, hanging candles and a mantle of wax-blackened feathers.

## Thren, God of the Gallows Hunt

**Champion** · Renn Nine-Nails

Renn's quarry can hear a nail driven into the god's gallows. The ninth nail means there is no trail home.

Forked stag antlers, a rough hide mantle and a hook-tipped longbow.

## Nereth, Goddess of the Black Current

**Champion** · The Ferryman Avar

Avar drowned without paying the fare. Nereth sent him back to drag others across the river in his place.

A drowned crown, ribbed shell collar and a trident covered in coins from the dead.

## Seryx, God of Broken Reflections

**Champion** · Vellis of the Cracked Face

Each shard around Vellis shows a life he traded away. Seryx promised to return one if he can remember which face was his.

Asymmetric obsidian visor and a broken halo of violet glass.

## Velmora, Goddess of Scarlet Tithes

**Champion** · Choirmaster Rhuun

He writes hymns in his own blood and buys a verse back whenever a listener bleeds first.

Crimson choir mitre, silver ribs and a chalice made to ring like a throat.

## Orun, God of the Last Toll

**Champion** · Hest of the Nine Bells

Orun rang once for the end of the world. Hest carries the remaining bells and decides who hears them next.

Bell-shaped helm, black-bronze skirt plates and funeral bells suspended from the back.

## Azkhar, God of the Pyre

**Champion** · Zarya Cindervein

Zarya escaped her own pyre dancing. Azkhar lets her live until the flames reach the soles of her feet again.

Angular ash plates, ember crown and a pair of hooked flame blades.

## Ossreth, God of the Pale Record

**Champion** · Eive Bonequill

Eive writes the names of the dying underneath the ice. Scratching one out makes the cold come looking for a replacement.

Glacial crown, iron-backed vellum and an elongated bone quill.

## Nhalume, Goddess of the Waking Crypt

**Champion** · Mara Unwaking

Mara walks inside the dreams of soldiers who died with their eyes open. The sheep bells she carries tell them which way home lies.

Crescent cage, layered mourning veil and a cold moon trapped above the staff.

## Kharoth, God of the Broken Shackle

**Champion** · Tavren the Unchained

Tavren broke out of Kharoth's prison. The god bound itself to the chain still wound around his arm and has followed him ever since.

Riveted manacles, jagged restraint plates and a dragging execution hook.

## Ulvane, God of the Buried Storm

**Champion** · Iskra Underbolt

Iskra extracted lightning from a dead god's spine. What answers her rods insists it was only sleeping.

Copper lightning rods, insulated black leathers and an illuminated spine of coils.

## Myrr, Goddess of the Last Decay

**Champion** · Nim of the Dust

Nim inherited wings from a plague saint. Every molt leaves behind a hunger that remembers who touched her.

Torn funerary wings, compound mask and antennae tipped with poison dust.
