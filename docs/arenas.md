# Lockwater arena guide

Every arena hosts the same first-to-three 3v3 match. A round lasts up to 60 seconds. The safe ring begins to contract with 25 seconds left. Each map gives both teams the same terrain mechanic and schedule. Cover can interrupt sight to a teammate as well as an opponent, so check where your healer stands before you round a pillar.


| Arena | What changes during a round | Time to react | Active duration | Repeats every |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lockwater Gatehouse | Two tidal pools on the flanks slow ground movement by 40% | 2 s | 5 s | 16 s |
| Copperbell Kiln | Two furnace vents deal 10 damage per second inside their circles | 2 s | 3 s | 12 s |
| Ironleaf Orchard | Two gusts push occupants in opposite directions at 3 m/s | 2 s | 3 s | 12 s |
| Shale Quarry | Two slabs rise into solid cover, displacing occupants to an edge | 2 s | 6 s | 16 s |
| Hollowglass Observatory | Two walkable veils obstruct sight across their boundaries | 2 s | 6 s | 16 s |

On all five maps, the first warning appears four seconds into each round, when the match clock reads **56**. The first active window starts at **54**. The floor outline marks the footprint. The HUD names the active terrain change. A higher tone marks the warning and a lower one marks activation. Press **M** to toggle sound. Cooldowns and the shrinking ring keep running through each cycle.

## Lockwater Gatehouse

Two offset stone pillars divide a circular platform in front of the lock towers. The open diagonal gives a ranged fighter a firing angle. Taking a tighter line around a pillar gives a pressured healer a place to break sight mid-exchange. A rising tide slows ground movement inside the two marked flank pools. Leaving the pool immediately restores normal terrain speed.

**Team play.** A Breakwater can hold a pillar corner while a Longwatch takes the opposite angle. The healer should keep a view of both before the attack starts. Against that formation, move the focused teammate around the far pillar and make the shooter cross open ground to follow. Before high tide, choose the dry route around the pillar or cross the pool while your movement is still unrestricted.

## Copperbell Kiln

The copper chimneys and furnace mouths frame a square fighting area with clipped corners and two furnace vents. A full three seconds standing in an active vent costs 30 health. Watch your exit as the warning begins, particularly when the safe ring is closing.

**Team play.** A Forgecaller can place a damage field beside a vent to pressure one exit. A Bellwarden can knock an opponent toward the vent during its active window. Against them, leave sideways during the warning and save your dodge for the opponent waiting at the edge.

## Ironleaf Orchard

Tree trunks give cover where the paths cross on the hexagonal orchard floor. The wind wheel marks the setting. Read the direction inside each gust circle separately. The wind can carry a melee fighter toward a target or pull a healer away from a teammate mid-exchange.

**Team play.** An Ash Dancer can approach along a favorable gust, then use Cross the Coals for the last stretch. A healer anticipating that approach can step out across the short side of the gust. A guard protecting an ally should move with them to keep the guard tether in range.

## Shale Quarry

Layered stone and a mine hoist frame the long, eight-sided quarry floor. Two marked slabs become solid obstacles when they rise. Fighters standing on a footprint move to the edge. The raised slabs interrupt both movement and sight until they retract.

**Team play.** A Threadkeeper who needs to finish Mend the Break can position on the same side of a rising slab as the wounded ally and use the slab to screen an enemy interrupter. Attackers can split their approach around opposite ends. Choose your side during the warning so the slab cannot split you from your healer.

## Hollowglass Observatory

Brass survey rings and an armillary sphere overlook the circular fighting floor. Each active veil has a marked perimeter. Fighters can walk through it. Two fighters inside the same veil can see one another, while a sightline that crosses its boundary is blocked.

**Team play.** A healer can follow a pressured ally into the same veil and force a distant attacker to change angles or enter. An Echo Duelist can wait near the perimeter to contest that entrance. If an ally is trying to heal you from outside, stay on their side of the boundary or cross it together.

## Reading a crowded fight

Check the map footprint first, then the fighter and their target. A filled floor effect can last after a cast animation finishes. A line between two fighters identifies the endpoints of an effect. A shield arcs around its recipient, a heal rises from the fighter receiving it, and a dash leaves a short ribbon along the movement path. In a practice round, stand beside a marked area and watch one complete warning and active window before trying a class combo there.

Map landmarks, floor outlines and directional markings give players cues alongside team and effect colors. For playtests, verify that a fighter can distinguish a map warning, a friendly field and an enemy cast when they overlap. Check visibility from both spawn directions and under the closing ring before using the layout in competition.
