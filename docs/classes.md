# Gods Die Here class guide

All 16 classes enter at full strength. Each has eight active abilities, a class resource and two optional masteries. Every fighter starts a round with 40 of 100 resource. Masteries exchange resource cost for recovery time on one spell. Preview and lock your choice before entering the arena.

A stun stops actions and movement. A root stops walking and the universal dodge. Class movement abilities can still reposition a rooted fighter. Silence blocks abilities on keys 2–8. Break Free clears these three control effects once per round.

| Role | Classes |
|---|---|
| Guard | Bellwarden, Breakwater, Forgecaller |
| Healer | Blood Cantor, Dream Shepherd, Lamplighter, Threadkeeper |
| Melee damage | Ash Dancer, Chainrunner, Duskmoth, Echo Duelist |
| Ranged damage | Coilwright, Glassmage, Longwatch, Sluicecaller, Winter Scribe |

## Breakwater

Shield tank · 140 health · Resolve

Strike, Brace, Take the Weight and Shield Check build Resolve. Keelbreaker hits harder while your guard tether is in range.

**Play the exchange.** Guard the teammate the other team is focusing with Take the Weight. Stay close enough to empower Keelbreaker. Save Shield Check for the cast that would turn the exchange.

**Against this class.** Flank Brace or draw the tank away from its guarded ally. Step outside Holdfast and re-engage when its ward expires.
